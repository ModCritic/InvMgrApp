InvMgr Privacy Policy

For InvMgr on Android. Last updated September 26, 2026.

InvMgr collects nothing. It makes no network connections, shows no ads, and has no analytics or tracking. It asks for no permissions.

Your rooms are kept on this device. They leave it only when you save or export a file and move it off this device yourself. Uninstalling the app deletes everything it stored.

Feedback you choose to send leaves through your own mail app or your own GitHub account, not through InvMgr.
